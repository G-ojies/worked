Privacy Policy

Worked · Last updated 27 September 2026

Worked is a study app. It is built to work with no internet connection, and it collects as little as it can.

What stays on your phone

Your revision progress, meaning which questions you marked shaky or got it, your streak, and the last question you opened, is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing progress in Settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

Purchases

Worked Plus is sold through Google Play, and purchases are managed by RevenueCat. When the app checks whether you have Worked Plus, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your purchase history for this app, and basic technical details such as app version, device model, operating system and country. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

See the RevenueCat privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

What we do not do

Worked has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It requests no device permissions. It does not collect your location, contacts, photos or files.

Children

Worked is written for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
