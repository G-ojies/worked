# Voiceover script

For `worked-demo.mp4`, 1 minute 45 seconds. Read at a normal speaking pace.
Each block is written to fit inside its time window with a breath to spare.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:06 | Home | I'm a computer science student in Nigeria. We revise from past questions, but nobody shares the answers. So I built Worked. |
| 0:06 to 0:15 | Course, then a question | Every question is a real exam question. You attempt it first. The solution stays hidden until you ask. |
| 0:15 to 0:26 | Worked solution | Then you see it worked, step by step. The diagram, the reasoning, the table, and a check at the end. All of it is on the phone, so it works with no signal. |
| 0:26 to 0:31 | Marking | One tap tells the app if you got it, or if it's still shaky. |
| 0:31 to 0:44 | Flashcards | Flashcards deal the shaky ones first. Flip the card, see the working, and mark it again. |
| 0:44 to 0:55 | Mock paper, then home | Mock papers are drawn from real past papers, timed by the marks. Then you mark yourself, strictly. |
| 0:55 to 1:01 | Locked sections | One course is completely free. For the others, the first two sections are free. |
| 1:01 to 1:16 | Paywall | The rest is Worked Plus. The plans match how students revise: monthly for the exam month, a semester plan, or lifetime. Prices come live from RevenueCat, so I can change them without a new release. |
| 1:16 to 1:30 | Purchase | Purchases run through RevenueCat. This is their Test Store. One entitlement, called Plus, unlocks everything. |
| 1:30 to 1:45 | Unlocked content | And it's open. Plus keeps working offline, because access is cached on the device. That's Worked: past questions, solved end to end. |

## Recording tips

- Record on your phone in a quiet room, holding it about a hand's width from your mouth.
- Play the video with the sound off while you read, so your pace follows the screen.
- One take for the whole script is easier to line up than ten short clips.
- Save it as `voiceover.m4a` or `voiceover.mp3`.
